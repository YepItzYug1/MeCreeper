# Privacy Policy for MeCreeper

**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MeCreeper development team ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the **MeCreeper** Discord bot ("the Bot"). 

By inviting MeCreeper to your server or using its commands and features, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
To provide and improve our services, MeCreeper may collect and store the following data provided through the Discord API:
* **Identifiers:** Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
* **Server Configuration Data:** Custom prefixes, welcome/log channel settings, moderation configurations, and feature toggles set by server administrators.
* **Feature-Specific Data:** Data required for specific commands to function (such as virtual currency balances, inventory items, leveling/XP stats, warnings, or reminders).
* **Command Logs:** Basic usage metrics, command execution timestamps, and error logs used strictly for debugging and performance monitoring.

*Note: We do **not** read, store, or log private Direct Messages (DMs) or general server chat messages unless explicitly required for a user-initiated command (such as an auto-moderation filter or message logging feature enabled by a server admin).*

---

### 2. How We Use Collected Information
We use the information we collect strictly to:
* Operate, maintain, and deliver MeCreeper's features and commands.
* Save server-specific settings and user progress across sessions.
* Diagnose technical issues, fix bugs, and prevent abuse, spam, or exploitation of the Bot.
* Improve overall bot stability and user experience.

---

### 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
* **No Selling of Data:** We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Discord IDs to third parties.
* **Infrastructure Providers:** Your data may be stored on secure third-party cloud hosting and database providers solely for the purpose of running the Bot.
* **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests, or to enforce our Terms of Service and protect against harm.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Security
* **Retention:** Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Bot's services. If MeCreeper is removed from a server, server-specific configuration data is automatically deleted or queued for deletion within 30 days.
* **Security:** We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

### 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion
You have the right to request access to or deletion of the data associated with your Discord User ID or a server you own:
* **Opt-Out:** You can stop data collection at any time by discontinuing use of MeCreeper or kicking the Bot from your server.
* **Data Removal Requests:** To request the complete deletion of your stored user or server data, please contact us via our Support Server or Support Email listed below. We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
MeCreeper complies with Discord's Terms of Service and is not intended for use by individuals under the minimum age required to use Discord in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from underage users.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. Continued use of MeCreeper after any updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
